The Story

The World Is Full of Wonders

Gon Freecss grew up believing his father was dead — until he learned the truth: Ging Freecss is one of the world's greatest Hunters. Driven to find him, Gon takes the Hunter Examination, a brutal test that fewer than one in ten thousand ever survive.

Along the way he forges bonds with Killua, an assassin escaping his destiny; Kurapika, driven by the ghost of genocide; and Leorio, a man who wants to be a doctor to avenge the poor. Their paths spiral into conspiracies, wars between humans and Chimera Ants, and a revelation that rocks the foundations of what it means to be alive.

Hunter × Hunter is not a story about the strongest. It is a story about the most human.

Story Arcs

01

Hunter Exam Arc

EP 1–21

Where it all begins. Gon, Killua, Kurapika and Leorio meet during the brutal Hunter Examination — a multi-phase test of survival, intellect, and spirit that transforms strangers into brothers.

02

Zoldyck Family Arc

EP 22–26

To save Killua from his assassin family's compound, Gon ventures into Kukuroo Mountain — an estate guarded by butler-warriors whose weakest member can lift a 4-ton gate with one hand.

03

Heavens Arena Arc

EP 27–36

Gon and Killua train at the 251-floor battle tower where fighters wager their lives for prize money. Here they awaken their Nen — and encounter Hisoka for the first unforgettable time.

04

Yorknew City Arc

EP 37–58

The Phantom Troupe descend on Yorknew City to steal from the Underground Auction. Kurapika uses his Emperor Time to chain them — and his own lifespan pays for every second he uses it.

05

Greed Island Arc

EP 59–75

A rare game created by Ging Freecss himself becomes the next lead. Inside the game world — which is an actual physical island run by Nen — they hunt 100 specified cards to win and find clues to Ging.

06

Chimera Ant Arc

EP 76–136 · MAGNUM OPUS

The longest, darkest, most philosophically complex arc in anime history. A new species — the Chimera Ants — emerges with human intelligence and monstrous Nen. Their king, Meruem, may be god. And Gon loses everything trying to stop them.

The Power System

The Nen System

Nen is the life energy of all living things. Its mastery is what separates a Hunter from a legend. Six types — each with its own strengths, weaknesses, and personality alignment.

💪

Enhancement

GON · UVOGIN

Amplifies the user's natural abilities — strength, speed, healing. The most balanced and powerful type in raw combat. Simple but overwhelming.

⚡

Transmutation

KILLUA · HISOKA

Changes the properties of Nen aura to mimic another substance. Electricity, rubber, gum — the transmuter's imagination is the limit.

🔗

Conjuration

KURAPIKA · KITE

Materializes physical objects out of Nen. The most training-intensive type. Conjured items disappear if concentration breaks — but the constructs can be extraordinarily complex.

💥

Emission

LEORIO · RAZOR

Projects Nen aura away from the body. The further from the body, the weaker the aura — but an Emitter can fire sustained blasts across vast distances.

🎭

Manipulation

ILLUMI · SILVA

Controls living or non-living things using Nen. A Manipulator can seize command of enemies or objects — but control requires rules the user must follow precisely.

👑

Specialization

MERUEM · CHROLLO · NEFERPITOU

A sixth category that defies all classification. Specialist abilities are unique to the individual — abilities that steal, transform, or transcend the other five categories entirely.
